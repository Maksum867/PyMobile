# Code of Conduct

PyMobile welcomes people who want to build, use, document, test, and improve the project. This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces, including interactions that represent the project elsewhere (for example social media posts or conference talks made on behalf of the project).

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, ask questions, and make room for contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give useful feedback, credit others' work, and respect their time and privacy.
- If someone asks you to stop an unwelcome interaction, stop.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeatedly derailing discussions or targeting contributors is also not acceptable. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this code.

## Reporting concerns

If it is safe to do so, you may ask someone to change their behavior. You do **not** have to confront them before reporting a concern. For a private report, email the project maintainer at **m02956682@gmail.com** (the contact published in `pyproject.toml`). Include a link or description of the incident and any relevant context. Do not post sensitive personal details publicly.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md), not a public issue or conduct report.

## Handling reports

The maintainer will review reports as privately as practical, hear from the people involved when appropriate, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a reminder or warning, removing content, limiting participation, or banning an account from project spaces. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is needed to investigate or address harm; information will be shared only as necessary.

If you disagree with a moderation decision, you may ask the maintainer to reconsider by replying privately with additional context. There is no separate appeals body; the hosting platform's reporting tools remain available.

If the maintainer is involved in the concern, this project does not currently have an independent moderation team. In that case, use the relevant hosting platform's reporting tools. We do not promise a fixed response time.

## Working together

- Welcome questions and different levels of experience. Explain project
  conventions and decisions without belittling the person asking.
- In reviews, identify the concrete problem, explain its impact and suggest a
  constructive alternative where possible. Disagreement about a technical
  decision is acceptable; insults, intimidation and repeated personal criticism
  are not.
- Respect requests for reasonable accessibility accommodations and different
  communication needs. Give people space to participate without requiring them
  to disclose a disability or other private information publicly.
- Credit contributors accurately, respect consent and attribution, and do not
  present another person's work as your own. Do not publish private messages,
  contact details, screenshots or recordings without permission.
- Accept correction, take responsibility for mistakes, and adjust behaviour
  when asked. Avoid unwanted contact after someone asks to be left alone.

Examples of unacceptable conduct include discriminatory or sexualized comments,
slurs, stalking, unwelcome sexual attention, threats of violence, targeted
harassment, impersonation, disclosure of private information and encouraging
others to harass someone. This list is illustrative, not exhaustive. A pattern
of behaviour can be harmful even if each individual message seems minor.

## Making a report

A report can be brief and does not need to use a particular format. If available,
include:

- what happened and where, with links or message identifiers;
- who was involved and any relevant context or repeated behaviour;
- supporting material you are comfortable sharing, such as a screenshot;
- whether you have an immediate safety or privacy concern, and a safe way to
  reach you for follow-up.

Do not investigate the incident yourself, collect information you are not
entitled to access, or share someone else's sensitive information publicly.
Reports made in good faith are welcome even if evidence is incomplete. The
maintainer may ask questions but cannot promise anonymity, a specific outcome,
or a fixed response time. If there is an immediate threat, contact appropriate
local emergency or support services; project moderation is not an emergency
response service.

## Moderation principles

The maintainer should assess the available information fairly, consider the
safety and privacy of those affected, and use a response proportionate to the
behaviour and circumstances. A response may address a single incident or a
pattern; warnings are not required before every action. Moderation may include
removing content, restricting interaction, temporary limits or a ban in spaces
the project controls. Platform rules and platform enforcement remain separate.

The maintainer may not be able to disclose details of an investigation or action
to every participant. Reports and records should be shared only as needed to
address the concern, subject to applicable obligations; absolute confidentiality
and anonymity cannot be guaranteed. Do not retaliate against a reporter,
witness, respondent or anyone participating in a good-faith process.

For reconsideration, reply privately with relevant information that may have
been missed. This is a request to the same maintainer, not an independent appeal.
If the maintainer is involved or you are uncomfortable approaching them, use
the hosting platform's reporting mechanisms; the project currently has no
independent moderation team. You may also seek help from an appropriate external
service. This policy does not limit any rights or options outside project spaces.
