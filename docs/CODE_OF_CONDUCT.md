# Code of Conduct

PyMobile welcomes people who want to build, use, document, test, and improve the project. This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces, including interactions that represent the project elsewhere.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, ask questions, and make room for contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give useful feedback, credit others' work, and respect their time and privacy.
- If someone asks you to stop an unwelcome interaction, stop.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeatedly derailing discussions or targeting contributors is also not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

If it is safe to do so, you may ask someone to change their behavior. You do **not** have to confront them before reporting a concern. For a private report, email the project maintainer at **m02956682@gmail.com** (the contact published in `pyproject.toml`). Include a link or description of the incident and any relevant context. Do not post sensitive personal details publicly.

For security vulnerabilities, follow the separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md), not a public issue or conduct report.

## Handling reports

The maintainer will review reports as privately as practical, hear from the people involved when appropriate, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a reminder or warning, removing content, limiting participation, or banning an account from project spaces. Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is needed to investigate or address harm; information will be shared only as necessary.

If the maintainer is involved in the concern, this project does not currently have an independent moderation team. In that case, use the relevant hosting platform's reporting tools. We do not promise a fixed response time.
